What is PoliticAxes?

PoliticAxes is an expanded political quiz that attempts to assign percentages for seventeen different political axes. You will be presented with a series of statements, and for each, you will answer with your honest opinion, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree.

At the end of the quiz, your scores will be compared against 67 different ideologies to find your closest match. Answer honestly!

There are questions in the test.

What are the 19 axes?

PoliticAxes moves beyond the standard left-right spectrum by measuring 19 distinct dimensions of political and philosophical thought, grouped into four main categories:

GOVERNMENT & SOCIETY

Measures your views on Authority vs. Liberty, Democracy vs. Autocracy, and Federalism vs. Unitary

ECONOMICS & LABOR

Tracks views on Markets vs. Equality, Planned Economy vs Laissez-Faire, Collective vs Private Ownership, and the Ecological vs Industrial orientation of the Economic System.

GLOBAL & MILITARY

Covers Nationalism vs Globalism, Pacifiscm vs Militarism, Open vs Closed Borders, and Beliefs on Future Space Expansion

PHILOSOPHY & CULTURE

The philosophical axes: Progressivism vs Traditionalism, Secularism vs Religiosity, Multiculturalism or Cultural Assimilation, Utilitarianism Vs Deontic Ethics, Relativism Vs Absolutism, Beliefs in Utopianism vs Dystopianism, Existentialism (Nihilism vs Meaning), and Brain Rot Vs Classic.

What's the "Closest Match" mean?

The quiz uses Euclidean Distance to map your coordinates against 67 pre-defined ideologies. It finds the ideology with the smallest mathematical distance from your results (maths mumbo jumbo goes here hehe). This is for educational and entertainment purposes!
